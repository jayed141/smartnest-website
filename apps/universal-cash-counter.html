Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 21, 2026

SmartNest Technologies operates the Universal Cash Counter mobile application. Your privacy is critically important to us, and this policy explains how we handle user data.

1. Data Minimization & Privacy

Universal Cash Counter values user privacy above all else. The app does not collect, store, track, or share any personal, financial, or banking data. All transactional registries and calculations remain strictly on your local device hardware.

2. UI Components & Copyright Compliance

To ensure complete copyright compliance and safety, this application does not use realistic currency images or third-party proprietary assets. All note and coin visuals are custom-designed vector graphics unique to SmartNest Technologies.

3. Local Secure Storage

Any session logs or cash counting histories are managed using a secure offline localized database (SQLite) built directly into the app structure. No data is ever synced or transmitted to any external cloud network or third-party server.
