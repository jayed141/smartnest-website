This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and protection of your information when you use our App.

By downloading, installing, and using HisabNest, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with these terms, please do not use the App.

1. Information Collection and Storage (Offline-First Architecture)

HisabNest is strictly an "Offline-First" application. We prioritize your privacy above all else.

  • Local Database Storage: All the data you enter into the App—including cash denominations, customer ledger records (Udhar), inventory items, shopping lists, and business profile details—is stored exclusively on your device's internal storage using a secure, local SQLite database.
  • No Cloud Servers: We do not collect, transmit, upload, sync, or store any of your personal, financial, or business data on our servers, third-party cloud servers, or any remote databases. Your data belongs entirely to you and never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export or share it.

2. Device Permissions and Usage

To provide certain features, HisabNest requires access to specific device permissions. We handle these strictly according to Google Play Developer Policies:

  • Camera and Photo Gallery: The App may request access to your device's camera or photo library. This is used solely to allow you to upload a company logo or signature stamp for your PDF invoices. These images are saved locally on your device and are never uploaded to the internet or any external servers.
  • Storage / File Access: The App requires storage permissions to securely generate PDF reports, save files, and allow you to export or import your database backup files. We do not access or read any other personal files on your device.

3. Sharing of Information

Because we do not collect or store your data on our servers, we have no data to sell, trade, rent, or share with third parties.

  • User-Initiated Sharing: The App contains features that allow you to share calculations, PDF invoices, and text statements with your customers or contacts. This sharing is entirely initiated and controlled by you using third-party applications installed on your device (e.g., WhatsApp, Email, SMS). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party services.

4. Data Security and Backup Recommendations

Since HisabNest operates completely offline, you are solely responsible for the safety, security, and backup of your data. If you uninstall the App, clear the App’s data, or lose your device, your data will be permanently deleted. We have no way to recover it for you.

  • Regular Backups: We strongly advise you to regularly use the "Backup Data (Export)" feature located in the App's Settings to secure your data.
  • Recommended Backup Method (Gmail): For the safest and most reliable backup experience, we highly recommend sharing/exporting your backup file directly to your Gmail account (e.g., emailing it to yourself). Storing the file as a Gmail attachment is a highly secure way to ensure your data is never lost and can be easily imported back into the App using the "Restore Data (Import)" option whenever needed.
