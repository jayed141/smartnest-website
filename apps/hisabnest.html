Privacy Policy - HisabNest

SmartNest Technologies Specific Disclosure

1. Data Collection & SQLite Hybrid Architecture

HisabNest is designed with user privacy as the core foundation. The application utilizes an offline-first hybrid SQLite database model to manage data efficiency. All your calculation histories, cash entries, and financial logs are stored strictly on your local device. We do not collect, upload, or sync any of your personal financial data to our servers or any third-party cloud infrastructure.

2. Copyright Protection & Visual Assets

To maintain the highest level of intellectual property compliance, HisabNest does not use any realistic or copyrighted currency images. The application interface exclusively relies on original, custom-designed vector graphics for all denomination representations, ensuring complete legal safety for our users.

3. Third-Party Access

Since the core functionality of HisabNest operates independently of internet servers, we do not integrate any third-party tracking SDKs or analytics tools that silently harvest your device data in the background.

4. Regular Policy Updates & User Responsibility

SmartNest Technologies reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time to reflect changes in legal regulations, Google Play policies, or app features.

It is the user's responsibility to periodically review this Privacy Policy for any updates. We strongly encourage you to regularly check this page on our website and within the app. Your continued use of HisabNest after any modifications indicates your acknowledgment and consent to the updated terms.
